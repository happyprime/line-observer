All demos. Each box's bar and dot follow its --scroll-progress: 0 when the line meets the top edge, 1 at the bottom edge. A box past the line holds 1, and one below it holds 0. Insert content above the page with B on the line: scroll anchoring moves the page, B stays put, and so does its offset. Grow C while it is on the line, then scroll: its progress counts the new height.
